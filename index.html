A family legacy · Addis Ababa

More than seven decades. Generations of collecting.

The story of Zebra Gallery’s collection reaches back more than 70 years, beginning with pieces gathered by the grandfather of Mr Tekle Tsehaye. Across generations, the family has continued to preserve and expand this remarkable collection of Ethiopian and African cultural objects.

3 · Axum style

Rounded and circular compositions are often associated with the Axumite design tradition.

Axum-Style Circular Cross

A gilded openwork cross head with a rounded, quatrefoil composition and a socket for a processional staff.

Museum reference; see source record for object details.

Public-domain image: The Metropolitan Museum of Art.

Enquire about this style

Axum-Style Openwork Cross

A second rounded openwork reference, showing the repeated cross motifs and circular structure associated with this design family.

Style reference; regional attribution describes design, not necessarily the object's place of manufacture.

Public-domain image: Smithsonian National Museum of African Art.

Enquire about this style
4 · Lalibela style

Lalibela cross designs include distinctive elongated and framed forms, alongside celebrated circular processional examples.

Lalibela — Saint George Cross

A processional cross associated with Lalibela, known as the Cross of Saint George, with concentric circular ornament and a staff socket.

Reference listing: approximately 28.5 cm high.

Image reference: Ethnic Design Collection.

Enquire about this style

Lalibela — Circular Openwork Cross

A circular processional form with a radiating border of small crosses and a central cross motif, documented in Ethiopian church art.

Museum reference: 14th–15th century.

Public-domain image: Smithsonian National Museum of African Art.

Enquire about this style

Diamond-shaped hand-cross compositions are a well-known Gondarian design reference.

A hand cross with a diamond-like openwork head, a straight handle and a square base, illustrating the geometry associated with Gondarian designs.
